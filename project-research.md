## ESA context (esasafe.com, public)
- Anyone hired to do electrical work in an Ontario home must be a Licensed Electrical Contractor (LEC).
- A notification of work is filed with ESA before work begins and creates a permanent record.
- ESA inspects at key stages (service, rough-in, final).
- A Certificate of Acceptance is issued when work passes; it matters for insurance and resale.
- Homeowners can verify a licence with ESA's online contractor locator.
Sources: https://esasafe.com/compliance/what-you-need-to-know/ , https://esasafe.com/compliance/hiring-a-licensed-electrical-contractor/
